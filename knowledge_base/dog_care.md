# Dog Care Guidelines

## Daily Exercise
- Adult dogs need 30-60 minutes of exercise per day, depending on breed.
- High-energy breeds (Border Collies, Huskies, Labs) need 60-120 minutes.
- Senior dogs (7+ years) benefit from 2-3 shorter walks (15-20 min) instead of one long walk.
- Puppies under 6 months should not be over-exercised; use the "5 minutes per month of age" rule.

## Feeding
- Adult dogs: 2 meals per day, spaced about 8-12 hours apart.
- Puppies under 6 months: 3-4 smaller meals per day.
- Always provide fresh water.
- Avoid feeding immediately before or after vigorous exercise (risk of bloat in large breeds).

## Grooming
- Short-haired dogs: brush 1-2 times per week.
- Long-haired dogs: brush daily to prevent matting.
- Bathe every 4-6 weeks unless dog gets dirty.
- Trim nails every 3-4 weeks.

## Medications
- Give medications at the same time each day for consistency.
- Heartworm and flea/tick preventatives are typically given monthly.
- Never skip doses of prescription medications without consulting a vet.

## Mental Enrichment
- Dogs need mental stimulation as much as physical exercise.
- Puzzle toys, training sessions, and sniff walks are great enrichment.
- 10-15 minutes of training per day strengthens the bond and tires them mentally.
