# Cat Care Guidelines

## Feeding
- Adult cats: 2 meals per day OR free-feed dry food + 1-2 wet food meals.
- Kittens under 6 months: 3-4 small meals per day.
- Cats are obligate carnivores; meat-based diet is essential.
- Fresh water daily; many cats prefer running water (fountains).

## Litter Box Care
- Scoop the litter box at least once per day.
- Full litter change every 1-2 weeks.
- Rule of thumb: one litter box per cat, plus one extra.
- Place boxes in quiet, accessible locations away from food.

## Play and Enrichment
- Cats need 2-3 play sessions per day, 10-15 minutes each.
- Use wand toys, laser pointers, or puzzle feeders.
- Indoor cats especially need enrichment to prevent boredom and obesity.
- Vertical space (cat trees, shelves) is important for territorial security.

## Grooming
- Short-haired cats: brush 1-2 times per week.
- Long-haired cats (Persians, Maine Coons): brush daily to prevent matting.
- Most cats groom themselves and rarely need baths.
- Trim nails every 2-3 weeks.

## Health
- Senior cats (10+ years) should have wellness checks twice per year.
- Watch for changes in eating, drinking, or litter box habits — these are early signs of illness.
- Cats hide pain well; behavior changes are often the first warning sign.
