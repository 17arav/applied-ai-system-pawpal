# Daily Routine Best Practices

## Why Routine Matters
- Pets thrive on predictability — it reduces anxiety and behavior issues.
- Consistent timing for meals, walks, and meds improves digestion, sleep, and overall health.
- Routine also helps owners stay accountable.

## Morning (6-9 AM)
- Bathroom break / walk (dogs).
- Breakfast.
- Fresh water refill.
- Quick play or training session (5-10 min).

## Midday (11 AM - 2 PM)
- Bathroom break (especially for puppies, seniors, small dogs).
- Optional short walk or play session.
- Mental enrichment (puzzle toy, chew).

## Afternoon (3-6 PM)
- Main exercise session (longer walk, fetch, run).
- Training or socialization.

## Evening (6-9 PM)
- Dinner.
- Calm play or grooming session.
- Final bathroom break before bed.

## Spacing Tasks
- Leave at least 30 minutes between meals and vigorous exercise.
- Don't bunch all tasks into one short window — spread them out.
- Medications generally work best on a fixed schedule (same time daily).

## Multi-Pet Households
- Feed pets separately if there's any food guarding.
- Ensure each pet gets individual attention each day.
- Watch for one pet hogging exercise time at another's expense.

## Time Budgeting
- A typical adult dog needs ~90-120 min of total daily care (walks + feeding + grooming + play).
- A typical adult cat needs ~30-45 min of total daily care.
- Senior pets often need MORE time, not less, due to medications and slower pace.
